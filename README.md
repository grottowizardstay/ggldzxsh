# Legends of Seltso

Fabric mod for Minecraft 1.21.1 (formerly "Kalabushkin"). The mod id is still `kalabushkin`, so existing worlds keep working.

- **Kalabushkin** fist-bumps you, drops a carpenter's plane or mallet and leaves a spirit that follows you.
- **Vlad Logutov** rides his mountain bike. When he sees a player he rides up, puts a foot down and asks his question. He drops the **Shatuni**, a two-handed crankset: hold use to spin it like a circular saw.
- **Little Koch** is 1.4 blocks tall, appears together with Vlad and keeps close to him. He drops fat, which can be eaten.
- **Vasily** shambles around like a zombie with his arms out, holding snus, a vape, a cigarette or a Dyadkovo cigarette. Interact with him and he shakes your hand, does a bit with the item and hands it over. Using any of them gives severe weakness, nausea and the Trip effect.
- **Barsik** is a big, fat tabby cat that cannot be hurt. He runs for the most sheltered spot nearby and loafs there until a player comes close. If he falls more than 3 blocks he puts on aviator goggles, opens a parachute and lands softly.
- **Alexey** (1.7 blocks) and **Rita** (1.6 blocks) walk together. Rita never makes a sound; every so often Alexey stops and says one of seven compliments to her. He drops a phone in a diamond case: hold use to fire a stream of gifts from it. Most are roses (2 damage); about one in 25 is a diamond (12 damage).
- **Darina** (2.2 blocks) is big and always peckish. Every minute or three her hunger wins: she rubs her belly for a few seconds as a warning, then goes for the nearest mob or survival player within 16 blocks, grabs it with both hands, lifts it to her mouth and eats it. Throw cooked meat (beef, pork, chicken, mutton or rabbit) on the ground near her and she eats that instead: each piece keeps her calm for two minutes, and she drops whoever she is holding to get it. Hitting her makes her come for you, fed or not. When she dies everything she swallowed comes back out: mobs alive, eaten players' belongings and the meat as items.

Build with `./gradlew build` (Java 21). Textures and the mod icon are generated by `python3 tools/generate_textures.py` and `python3 tools/generate_weapons.py` (need Pillow).
